How to Play

1

Choose a Mode

Select a difficulty level or create your own custom number range.

2

Start the Game

Click the Start Game button to generate a hidden number.

3

Make Your Guess

Enter a number within the selected range and click Guess.

4

Use the Hints

"Too High" means your guess is above the hidden number. "Too Low" means it is below.

5

Try Again

Once you find the number, use Play Again to start another round.
